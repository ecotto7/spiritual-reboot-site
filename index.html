Hey, I'm Elliot.

I'm not a pastor, and I don't have formal biblical training. Not that long ago, I barely read the Bible. The Word felt familiar, but not alive.

Then I got invited to a church that reads entire books of Scripture, line by line, word for word. Something stirred in me. Curiosity turned into hunger, and the questions haven't stopped since.

Spiritual Reboot is my testimony of that journey. My hope isn't to teach you what to believe. It's that you might get curious with me and open the Word for yourself.

Wherever you are in your journey, you're welcome here.
